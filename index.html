Selected Work

Exact Robustness Verification for Spiking Neural Networks

Star-set reachability, branch-and-bound, linear programming

An exact robustness verifier for trained convolutional first-to-fire spiking neural networks, combining star-set reachability with property-directed branch-and-bound. A property-directed pruning strategy confines branching to the firing decisions capable of realizing a pending violation, cutting search nodes, LP queries, and runtime by 8–26× with provably identical verdicts. Benchmarked on 20 MNIST queries across six perturbation radii and two threat-model sizes, delivering complete verdicts through ε = 0.10 (k = 1) and ε = 0.07 (k = 2); presented as a poster and a technical talk.

AlphaZero Reinforcement Learning for Connect Four and Backgammon

Python, MCTS, reinforcement learning

An AlphaZero-style self-play system with a dual-headed policy–value network and PUCT-guided Monte Carlo Tree Search, reaching a 95%+ win rate against a random Connect Four baseline. To handle stochastic games, I proposed and implemented Expecti-MCTS, a variant that interleaves player decision nodes with probabilistic chance nodes, then adapted the agent to Backgammon with Chance-Aware MCTS that branches explicitly over dice-roll outcomes — outperforming standard MCTS baselines with a 75% win rate.

English Football Player Market Value Prediction

Python, XGBoost, MLP, GNN

A valuation pipeline that classifies player-season observations into market-value tiers from tabular features, categorical embeddings, and temporal season-based splits. Modeling club-season teammate relationships as a graph enables GraphSAGE message passing to capture relational team-context signals beyond isolated player statistics. Across XGBoost, MLP, MLP + ClubID, and teammate-edge GraphSAGE, the GNN achieved the best test performance at 0.580 accuracy and 0.550 macro-F1.
